## What it's about

A Create-centric kitchen-sink modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1. Instead of just stacking
mods, the pack gets its own identity: custom items and fluids via KubeJS, customized
world generation and a 58-part quest progression that guides you through the
mechanics.

## Technology

The custom content is built entirely in KubeJS scripts — no separate Java mod
needed. Recipes are adjusted pack-wide so the mods don't trivialize each other and
the Create machines stay at the center of the progression.

## Workflow

Developed with an AI as pair programmer against KubeJS's API friction: small
commits, descriptive alpha versions, every change tested in-game. The repo is
public — scripts, quests and configuration are there to read.

## Status

The pack hasn't been released yet. The release is planned for CurseForge: some
FTB mods may not be distributed via Modrinth, which rules out a release there.
